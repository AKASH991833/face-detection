FaceTrace

See what a neural net sees

A Tiny-YOLO face detector plus a 68-point landmark network run on your device. Point your camera or drop a photo - nothing ever leaves the browser.

Start the webcam or load an image
Honest limits. Detection uses the tiny_face_detector network - fast and solid on frontal faces up to a few meters; it will miss extreme angles, heavy occlusion, and tiny distant faces. Landmarks are 68-point and approximate. All inference is local; no frame or photo is uploaded. Webcam needs browser permission (HTTPS/localhost).
